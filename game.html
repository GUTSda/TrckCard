How To Play

Overview

Battle against AI using a deck of 5 or 10 cards. Defeat all enemy cards to win!

Elements (+15 bonus dmg)

🔥 Fire → beats 🌿 Nature
🌿 Nature → beats 🟤 Ground
🟤 Ground → beats 💧 Water
💧 Water → beats 🔥 Fire

Turn System (RPS Winner-Only)

Every exchange starts with Rock-Paper-Scissors. Only the winner acts. The loser does nothing that turn. After the winner acts, RPS rerolls immediately. This repeats until the battle ends.

Actions (Winner Only)

  • Attack — Basic damage + element bonus
  • Skill — Powerful ability (has cooldown)
  • Switch — Swap active card (uses your action)
  • Use Buff — Attach a buff card to active unit

Deck Modes

  • 5-Card Mode: 5 cards, min 2 Active
  • 10-Card Mode: 10 cards, min 6 Active

Card Types

  • Active: Fighters with HP, ATK, and skills
  • Passive: Auto-buff allied element cards
  • Buff Attachment: Equip mid-battle for bonus

Coins

Win = 100–500 coins. Lose = 40 coins. Complete quests for big rewards!
