How To Play

Overview

Battle against an AI using a deck of 5 cards. Defeat all enemy active cards to win!

Elements & Counters (+15 bonus dmg)

🔥 Fire → beats 🌿 Nature
🌿 Nature → beats 🟤 Ground
🟤 Ground → beats 💧 Water
💧 Water → beats 🔥 Fire

Turn System

Every round starts with Rock-Paper-Scissors. The winner acts first. Both players perform one action each round, then a new RPS begins.

Actions

  • Basic Attack — Deal damage (+ element bonus if applicable)
  • Use Skill — Powerful ability with a cooldown
  • Switch Card — Swap your active card (uses your action)

Deck Building

  • Deck size: exactly 5 cards
  • Minimum 2 active cards required
  • Passive cards auto-apply bonuses to matching element cards

Coins

Win battles to earn coins. Use coins to summon new cards from the Gacha Shop!
